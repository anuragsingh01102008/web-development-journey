Welcome, Detective

A legendary diamond has disappeared.

Your mission is to discover who took it, how they took it, and where it went.

Case Information🔎

CASE:The Moonstone Mystery

LOCATION:Verinder Estate, Yorkshire

TIME:After Rachel Verinder's birthday celebration

MISSING ITEM:The Moonstone-a legendary diamond

Suspects🕵️

  • Rachel Verinder - The owner of the diamond
  • Franklin Blake - Rachel's cousin and admirer
  • Rosanna Spearman - A servant at the estate
  • Godfrey Ablewhite - A respected family friend
  • The Three Indian Guardians - Mysterious men searching for the diamond

Evidence📸

  1. The diamond disappeared from Rachel's bedroom.
  2. Several people had access to the house that night.
  3. Rachel suddenly became angry with Franklin Blake
  4. Rosanna behaved strangly after the disappearance.
  5. A suspicious mark was discovered on clothing.
  6. Mysterious men had been following the diamond.
  7. The diamond seemed to have disappeared without anyone seeing it being taken

Case Solved🎯

The Truth

Your investigation has uncovered the truth....

The diamond was not simply stolen in the way everyone believed.

Franklin Blake had unknowingly taken the diamond while under the influence of a drug and later encountered Godfrey Ablewhite. Ablewhite secretly kept the diamond for himself and used it as security for money.

Mystery Solved

The mystery eventually reveals that the person who appeared respectable was hiding the darkest secret.
