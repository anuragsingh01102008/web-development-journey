How to make French Fries

Making French Fries is not such a hard task we just need to do a simple process.You guys just follow me and have the ingredients that we need.

Ingredients

  • Potatoes
  • Oil
  • Salt
  • Masala

Instructions

  1. Cut and Soak the Potatoes
  2. Parboil the Potatoes
  3. Fry them(Slow cook)
  4. Final Fry and Season
